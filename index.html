How it works + cheat sheet

After a pot-fraction bet f is called, the pot grows by a factor of (1 + 2f). To go from pot P to all-in (pot P + 2S) over N streets:

f = ((1 + 2·SPR)1/N − 1) / 2

Flop → N = 3, Turn → N = 2. Shortcut: find the growth multiplier m with mN ≈ 1 + 2·SPR, then bet (m − 1)/2 pot.
